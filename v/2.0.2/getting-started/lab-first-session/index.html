Module 1 · Get started: the agent, models and a safe setup

Lab: decisions in your first sessions

Three situations, three levels. No right buttons to memorise. Show how you think when working with an agent.

For everyone⏱ 30 minGraded · 6 pt

How this lab works#

You will face three situations from the first weeks with Codex. There is no environment to set up and no AI involved. The point is how you decide: what you check before acting, what you let the agent do, and when you stop it.

  • Level 1 gives feedback after every decision. Experiment freely.
  • Level 2 gives feedback only at the end.
  • Level 3 adds a limited investigation budget and a live agent you may need to stop. The situation is ambiguous on purpose.

Retake any level as often as you like. Your best result counts, and every try gets a new variant. Each level unlocks after you score at least 70% on the previous one, or after two tries.

Before you start

Have the topics The Codex CLI, sandbox and approvals and Giving Codex context and completion criteria fresh in mind.
