Codex Navigator

A guided path through OpenAI's official Codex training, with use cases, practice labs and badge checkpoints for every role.

How this navigator works. The course follows OpenAI's official Codex training: the OpenAI Academy learning paths, the Codex docs, OpenAI's videos and the Cookbook. Each page tells you why a resource matters and what to watch for, adds use cases from real teams, and ends with a quiz or a lab. Typical rhythm: read the brief → open the official material → come back for the practice.

Find your topics. Every page says who it's for: everyone, technical specialists or non-technical specialists. Skip what isn't yours.

Badges. Modules end with a 🏅 checkpoint. Finish the OpenAI Academy course, pass its assessment (80% or more), and paste the badge link from Accredible's email. The page checks it and records your grade automatically. Academy is free; cards that need an account show a 🔑 badge.
