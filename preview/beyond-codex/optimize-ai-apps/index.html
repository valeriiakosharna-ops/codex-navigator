Module 6 · Elective: building agentic systems and AI applications

Optimize AI application performance

Judge how a deployed AI system behaves under real traffic, compare versions, spot regressions, and trade quality off deliberately against latency, throughput, reliability and cost. Confirmed with the official badge.

For technical specialists⏱ ~45 min in Academy + assessmentGraded · 1 pt

Why this matters#

After launch, every AI feature turns into a set of trade-offs. A faster model or a lower effort saves money and time, until quality drops for one group of users that nobody was watching. This course is about making those trade-offs deliberately, using production evidence instead of a benchmark run on your laptop.
