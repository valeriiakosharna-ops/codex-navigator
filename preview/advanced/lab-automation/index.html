Lab: decisions about automation

When to automate, how to run agents in parallel, and how to notice an automation that quietly went wrong.

⏱ 35 minNavigates: Codex 103: Advanced Workflows and Automation (OpenAI Academy)Graded · 6 pt

How this lab works#

Automation multiplies whatever you already have, good habits and bad ones alike. These scenarios test your judgement about what to automate, with which permissions, and how to notice when an automated agent does something it shouldn't.

Levels unlock one after another. Level 2 has delayed consequences: an early decision may only show its cost later.
