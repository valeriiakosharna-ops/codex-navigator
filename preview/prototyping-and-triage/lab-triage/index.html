Module 2 · Natural-language prototyping and requirement analysis

Lab: triage with judgement

Three AI-made reports, three levels. Find the invented facts, the missing information and the estimates that hide their assumptions before they reach the team.

For non-technical specialists⏱ 30 minGraded · 6 pt

How this lab works#

Each level shows a report that Codex produced. Click the lines that should not go to the team as they are, and from level 2 rate how serious each problem is. Some lines look suspicious but are fine. Flagging those costs points, just as a false alarm costs the team time.

No AI is involved: the reports are fixed, and the lab tests your judgement. Retake any level as often as you like. Your best result counts. Each level unlocks after you score at least 70% on the previous one, or after two tries.

Before you start

Keep the quality bar from Issue triage and human-reviewed estimates in mind: evidence for every item, guesses marked as guesses, nothing presented as a commitment.

What to take away#

  • Evidence or it didn't happen. An item that can't be traced to a source doesn't go to the team.
  • Guesses are labelled. "Probably caused by" is a hypothesis to check, not a finding.
  • Drafts stay drafts. Sizes and priorities become commitments only when people agree to them.
  • Automation fails quietly. A routine report needs a "what I could NOT check" line, and someone who reads it.
