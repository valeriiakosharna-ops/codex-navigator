Module 1 · Get started with Codex

Lab: decisions in your first sessions

Three situations, three levels. No right buttons to memorise. Show how you think when working with an agent.

⏱ 30 minNavigates: Codex 101: Introduction and Onboarding (OpenAI Academy)Graded · 6 pt

How this lab works#

You will face three situations from the first weeks with Codex. There is no environment to set up and no AI involved. The point is how you decide: what you check before acting, what you let the agent do, and when you stop it.

  • Level 1 gives feedback after every decision. Experiment freely.
  • Level 2 gives feedback only at the end and allows 2 attempts.
  • Level 3 gives one attempt and a limited investigation budget. The situation is ambiguous on purpose.

Each level unlocks after you score at least 70% on the previous one, or use up its attempts.

Before you start

Have the topics Your first session, safely and Asking a coding agent for work fresh in mind.
