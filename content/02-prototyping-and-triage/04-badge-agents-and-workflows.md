This module's official course is **Agents and Workflows**. It's about directing agents in
everyday work: giving context, defining outputs, setting boundaries, reviewing results and
improving the workflow over time. You'll practise on a real task of your own.
Pass the assessment with **80% or more** and Accredible emails you a badge. Paste the link below.
