## Why this matters

"It worked when I tried it" is the AI-application version of "tests pass on my machine". An
evaluation turns a handful of impressions into **evidence**: representative cases, a clear way to
score them, and a record that tells you whether a change made things better or worse.

It's the same discipline this course asked of Codex's work (evidence, not reassurance), applied to
your own AI features.
