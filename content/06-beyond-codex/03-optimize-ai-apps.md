## Why this matters

After launch, every AI feature turns into a set of trade-offs. A faster model or a lower effort
saves money and time, until quality drops for one group of users that nobody was watching. This
course is about making those trade-offs **deliberately**, using production evidence instead of a
benchmark run on your laptop.
